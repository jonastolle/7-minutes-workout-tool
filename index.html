    About the workout

    The 7 minute workout is a high-intensity circuit of 12 body-weight exercises, 30 seconds each with 10 seconds of rest, described by exercise physiologists Brett Klika and Chris Jordan.

    • Klika, B. & Jordan, C. (2013). High-intensity circuit training using body weight: maximum results with minimal investment. ACSM's Health & Fitness Journal, 17(3), 8–13.
    • Reynolds, G. (2013, May 9). The Scientific 7-Minute Workout. The New York Times.
